# BOM notes

- Rows 1 to 13, 18 and 19 match the callouts in `media/exploded.png`, the components table in `docs/02-concept.md` and the parts in `cad/src/model.py`. Rows 14 to 17 are not modelled.
- Every line is priced in USD for one prototype (TRL 3, 2026-09-25). Most prices are estimates for the part class; the lines checked against a live listing are noted below. No supplier has been selected or ordered from.
- Kit total: **$5,065** over 19 lines. Value-engineering target: USD 1,610 (`budget_usd` in `project.yaml`, a hypothetical control target, not a limit; Amish, 2026-10-01). Value-engineering target: USD 1,610. Estimated cost of the constructable design: USD 5,065 (USD 3,455 over the target). The total is checked by `docs/04-calcs/sizing.py` [K1].
- Design for construction (PLP-DDR-003, 2026-10-01): line 1 repriced from $90 to $130 (top plate with notch and hangers, lower jaw, drive arms, pivot pin), line 3 from $45 to $50 (enclosure feet), line 13 from $20 to $45 (cam shaft, cams, crank, link, lever, springs, saddles and straps), line 10 respecified (tube hoop with 0.9 m of safety edge), line 18 respecified (bracket on the hoop), and line 19 added (two rubber steering stops, $10).
- Line 1 (2026-10-02): the 5 mm top plate and two hollow tube pivots (one per drive arm, each with an M12 bolt through its bore) replace the 6 mm plate and the single solid pin; line 13: the lever is a 20 x 10 x 2 mm steel tube. These approved savings cut about 2.0 kg and change no price. Line 10: the safety edge is now 68 mm high (60 to 128 mm above the floor) so the scanner shelf clears it. Line 5: the driver specification adds safe torque off and a monitored controlled-stop input for the personnel stop (no price change assumed). Line 7: the lidar UART is dropped; the scanner's safety outputs go to the safety relay.
- Line 6, contactors: raised from $45 to $75 for a second 24 V main contactor in series, one per safety relay channel (PLP-DDR-002, O6).
- Line 2, hub motors: PLP-CAL-001 needs 25.5 N·m per motor at worst, so the spec is 30 N·m peak. Listed class prices: $165 for a 20/40 N·m, 36 to 48 V motor with no brake listed ([UU Motor SVB8S](https://www.uumotor.com/high-torque-500w-robot-8-inch-servo-hub-motor.html)) and $249 for a 40/80 N·m, 24/48 V motor ([ZLTECH 8 in](https://zltech-hubmotor.en.made-in-china.com/product/NOgmzpqvCLhr/China-Zltech-8inch-24V-48V-200rpm-300kg-Load-Gearless-Electric-DC-Agv-Direct-Drive-Wheel-Hub-Servo-Motor-with-Encoder-for-Forklift.html)). $200 each assumes a 24 V winding with a brake. The 16 N·m peak, $145 class ([UU Motor SVH8S](https://retail.uumotor.com/product/250w-8-inch-encoder-motor-svh8s/)) is too weak.
- Line 18, safety laser scanner (changed 2026-10-02): Amish decided that follow mode is a driverless truck function under ISO 3691-4, so the USD 69 Slamtec RPLIDAR C1 class lidar (not safety-rated) is replaced by a safety laser scanner for personnel detection. Basis: SICK nanoScan3 Core I/O NANS3-AAAZ30AN1 at USD 3,444.30 from [Lesman](https://www.lesman.com/nans3-aaaz30an1) (Type 3, PL d, SIL 2, 3 m protective field, 70 ms response, one OSSD pair, 106.6 x 80 x 117.5 mm including plug, 0.67 kg; [SICK data sheet](https://www.sick.com/media/pdf/9/79/979/dataSheet_NANS3-AAAZ30AN1_1100333_en.pdf)), plus about USD 11 for the 3 mm steel shelf. The cheaper Hokuyo UAM-05LP-T301 (listed new at USD 2,750.00 by [Next Day Automation](https://www.nextdayautomation.com/products/new-hokuyo-uam-05lp-t301-safety-laser-scanner-5m-range-270-protection-24vdc), a surplus dealer, and refurbished at USD 1,347.53 by [Radwell](https://www.radwell.com/Buy/HOKUYO%20AUTOMATIC%20CO/HOKUYO%20AUTOMATIC%20CO/UAM-05LP-T301)) has a 143 x 110 x 110 mm body and a scan plane 100 mm above its base, so it does not fit between the bumper and the enclosure. The scan plane height above the nanoScan3's base (about 40 mm) and its mounting holes are assumed until the part is bought.
- The donor manual pallet jack is not included. A new 27 x 48 in, 2,500 kg (5,500 lb) manual jack costs about $396 ([Global Industrial](https://www.globalindustrial.com/p/global-industrial-153-standard-duty-pallet-jack-truck-5500-lb-capacity-27-x-48-forks)).
- This BOM is a costed design list, not a purchasing list. Buying parts is TRL 4 work and is on hold by Amish's instruction.
